# Royal Scepter Computers & Video Games - Shop Policies

## Payment
- We accept bank transfer and cash. POS available in-store.
- Full payment required before delivery.

## Delivery
- Within Lagos mainland: N2,000 - N3,500 (1-2 days)
- Within Lagos island: N3,000 - N4,000 (1-2 days)
- Outside Lagos: N5,000+ (3-5 days, depends on location)
- Orders above N500,000 get free delivery within Lagos.

## Warranty
- All laptops: 6 months warranty on used/refurbished, 12 months on brand new.
- All smartphones: 12 months warranty.
- Warranty covers manufacturing defects only. Physical damage (screen crack, water damage) is not covered.
- Keep your receipt - it is required for any warranty claim.

## Returns
- 7 days return if the item is faulty or not as described.
- Item must be in the same condition with all packaging and accessories.

## Opening hours
- Monday - Saturday: 8:30am - 7:00pm
- Sunday: Closed

## Contact
- WhatsApp: available 24/7, replies during business hours.
